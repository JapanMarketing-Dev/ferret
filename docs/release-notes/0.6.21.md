Ferret 0.6.21

- The project sidebar is simpler: it lists projects, with each product folder under its group, and nothing else. Sort order and "Starred only" are under the filter button next to +.
- Reviews moved to the Findings page. On the left are New Review (⌘⇧R), Import Meeting and your review history with search and filters; the findings of the selected review are on the right. For a group of products, "Review all products in one recording" and "Check pending items in turn" are on its dashboard, as before.
- Fixed: crash reports from the development build on Windows and Linux were dropped.
