Ferret 0.8.23

- Shared feedback now carries its images. Each piece of feedback keeps the screenshot of the state it was about, with the area the person outlined, so agents on your other PCs and your teammates' agents can open the image and see exactly which screen and part it meant. The web page (account.ferretade.dev) shows the images too; click one to enlarge it.
- What you type to AI agents counts as well, not only feedback given in Ferret. Ferret reads the instructions you gave Claude Code, Codex, Antigravity CLI, Gemini CLI and Qwen Code in your registered projects, finds what you keep asking for, and puts it at the top of the list your agents read, so you have to repeat yourself less. Slash commands, short replies such as "ok", and text Ferret itself sent are left out, and keys and passwords are masked before anything is shared.
