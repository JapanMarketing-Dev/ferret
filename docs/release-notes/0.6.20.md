Ferret 0.6.20

- Publish now posts for you. Write what you want to share and press Write with AI: the AI writes an X post and a blog article (Markdown) and picks images and videos from anywhere in the project folder, preferring files whose names match what you wrote. Edit the X text and the article in their own tabs, then post.
- Post to X with the X API. Under Connections, paste the OAuth 2.0 Client ID of your own X developer app (type Native App, callback `http://127.0.0.1:47815/x/callback`) and press Connect with X; you log in to X in your browser and allow access. Posts go out directly, with images or a video and as a thread when the text is longer than one post. Posting uses your X developer account's pay-per-use credits. The built-in-browser method from 0.6.19 is gone.
- Post to your blog. Add a WordPress site (user name and an Application Password) or an EmDash site (an API token with content and media access); Ferret checks the connection before saving it. The article is published (or saved as a draft) with its images uploaded; place them with `![caption](file)`, and the first image becomes the featured image.
- Connection secrets are stored encrypted with the system keychain and never shown on screen. Each draft keeps a record of where it was posted, with links.
- Fixed: AI prompts could be confused by a `<` in your note or in the material.
