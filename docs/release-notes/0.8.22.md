Ferret 0.8.22

- Sign in with your email address (Settings → Ferret account). Ferret sends a 6-digit code; there is no password. Once signed in, your feedback is shared across your PCs, and the past-feedback list your agents read includes what you said on your other PCs, so you have to repeat yourself less.
- Teams. On the web at account.ferretade.dev you can sign in the same way, create a team and invite people by email. Your agents then also read your teammates' feedback, so the whole team gets fewer repeated corrections.
- Only the project name, where the feedback was given, its title, date and text are sent. File paths on your PC are not sent. Sign out to stop sharing.
