Ferret 0.7.3

- Business Research: the goal is now one answer, where to enter. A new first request, "Decide where to enter", has the agent compare every candidate and keep researching until conclusion.md names exactly one market, with a reason for each candidate not chosen, the evidence behind it, a first market experiment and a runner-up. Every other request and the folder's CLAUDE.md / AGENTS.md now end by updating that conclusion instead of reporting the research log.
- With Claude Code, a stop hook in the research folder keeps the agent going until conclusion.md meets those conditions (up to 50 continuations per session). It only acts in sessions that received "Decide where to enter". Gates are still decided by you.
- Business Research: files opened from the file tree now open inside the research view, as tabs next to the research folders, so you can read and edit them without leaving it.
- Research folders now appear in the project list on the left. Choosing one opens Business Research; choosing another project leaves it.
