Ferret 0.7.0

Ferret now runs a business from research to daily posting, not only software development.

- Business Research (new, in the mode switch at the top): collect candidate markets with your terminal agent (Claude Code, Codex and others), keep each piece of evidence with its URL, quote, dates, facts and inferences apart, and judge each candidate by five conditions (problem, customer and buying moment, reason to switch, reach, revenue) with supporting and refuting evidence. Gates, a funnel (1,000 → 100 → 20 → 5 → 1–2), unit economics (CAC, LTV with several retention scenarios, free-to-paid break-even) and market experiments are built in. Only you can pass a gate; an agent writing "pass" into a file is ignored.
- Make it a business: a candidate that clears the gates becomes a business folder with its evidence, hypothesis, experiments and execution conditions carried over, registered as a project in your orchestra.
- Business tab: in a business folder, follow ten steps from target to the next improvement, send each step's prompt to the agent, and approve anything that leaves your Mac.
- Videos (new, in the mode switch): record a screen region, cameras and microphones on separate tracks; when you stop, the agent edits the video with the bundled ferret-video command (sync, transcription, cuts, captions, checks, export, thumbnail and post text); review it by talking and drawing on the preview. Posting to YouTube and TikTok is included but still being verified.
- The knowledge behind the research (customer acquisition principles, how to research and judge evidence) ships with Ferret and is given to the agent.
