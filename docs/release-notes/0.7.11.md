Ferret 0.7.11

- Fixed: on Windows, pressing Stop could leave Ferret stuck in "Recording". Stopping now always finishes, and the findings are built from everything recorded up to that point, even if one part of the recording does not respond.
- Fixed: what you said could go missing from the findings. A part that could not be transcribed now shows up as a finding marked "Needs check" (with its time range), anything the organizer leaves out is added as a finding with your own words, and one-character utterances can be restored from the excluded list.
- If the findings cannot be built after a recording, Ferret now tells you the recording is saved and can be rebuilt from the review list.
- Stopping a recording returns the findings sooner when transcription runs on the CPU.
- Ferret now has two modes only: Editor and Feedback. Business research, video making (ferret-video), and creating a business from "Add Project" have been removed. Folders you added with them stay in the project list as plain folders.
- "Add Project → Clone from GitHub" no longer lists GitLab projects. You can still clone any repository by pasting its URL.
- Fixed: agent-to-agent requests stopped working after Windows cleaned up Ferret's temporary folder while Ferret had been running for a long time.
- Opening a review with nothing left to restore now just shows the reason instead of being reported as a crash.
